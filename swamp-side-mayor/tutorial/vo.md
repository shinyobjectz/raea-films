Made from tutorial.script.ts by tools/vo.py; change the lines there. One narrator, dry and measured. Record a
chapter as one take with a two-second pause between lines, or one file a line named by its ID (03-05.wav).

## 01 · The map. THE GIANT MONITOR

**01.01** Down the left is the rail: your projects, each with its emoji. Most of them watch your pointer.

**01.02** That one's a duck. It isn't looking. Goose doesn't care for ducks.

**01.03** A project opens to its chat, where you talk to its agent: the AI doing the work.

**01.04** Beside it, the panel, open on the feed: everything the agent makes. Command-backslash hides it.

## 02 · Connect your AI. THE THREE DOORS

**02.01** The agent needs an AI to run on. At the foot of the rail are three doors. Goose went through Connections.

**02.02** *(patiently)* Goose went through Connections.

**02.03** In Connections, under Agents, connect the AI you already pay for, like Claude or ChatGPT.

## 03 · Start the project. THE CHAT

**03.01** The plus in the rail starts a project. Goose named it, and picked an emoji.

## 04 · Open what comes back. THE FEED

**04.01** What the agent makes lands in the feed: a still, a cut, a question it needs answered.

**04.02** Press one, and it opens full size.

**04.03** Escape, or Back, takes you back to where you were.

## 05 · Point at it. VERSION THREE

**05.01** By version two, the wings were still planks.

**05.02** Goose pressed Comment, clicked on the wing, and said so.

**05.03** That's a note, pinned to the exact spot. Edit sends it to the agent; Note keeps it for you.

**05.04** Send, and your notes become the agent's plan in Tasks, each task pointing back at its note.

**05.05** The fix comes back as the next version. Compare, in the version menu, wipes between them.

**05.06** The old one stays, in case you miss the planks.
