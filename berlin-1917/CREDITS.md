# Berlin, Winter 1917: credits

The film credits its records on screen as it shows them. Here they are in one place, with the tools and the people
whose work it stands on. ASSETS.md has each file's licence.

## The records

- **The photographs**: the Allgemeine Ortskrankenkasse der Stadt Berlin's housing survey, published 1901–1920 as
  *Unsere Wohnungs-Enquete* and *Unsere Wohnungsuntersuchungen*. Flash photographs by Heinr. Lichte & Co. The film is
  built on plate z2_1917_MP_0075, the Stube and Kammer of the porter's flat at Ritterstraße 120, photographed on
  18 October 1917, and its second plate, z2_1917_MP_0077, the cooking room. Zentral- und Landesbibliothek Berlin,
  public domain.
- **The address book**: *Berliner Adreßbuch* 1917, Zentral- und Landesbibliothek Berlin (PPN 34115495_1917), public
  domain.
- **The sewers**: James Hobrecht, *Die Canalisation von Berlin* (1884), atlas, e-rara, ETH-Bibliothek Zürich,
  DOI 10.3931/e-rara-19774, public domain.
- **The map**: *Straubes Übersichtsplan von Berlin*, 1910, 1:4,000, and the Geoportal Berlin's layers listed in
  `map/SOURCES.md` (Senatsverwaltung für Stadtentwicklung, Bauen und Wohnen; Landesdenkmalamt Berlin),
  dl-de/zero-2.0.
- **Names and lines dated to 1917**: OpenHistoricalMap contributors (CC0): street names outside the 1900 survey's
  coverage, U-Bahn stations, tram lines.
- **Other sources the narration cites**: Museum Neukölln, on the sick fund's 1922 survey; the research notes behind
  every fact are in `research/` and `docs/narration.md`, which gives each line's source.
