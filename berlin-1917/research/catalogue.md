# The catalogue: Berlin in winter 1917-18, outdoors and in

Generated by tools/refs/tables.ts from research/catalogue/*.ts. Read research/outdoor.md and research/interiors.md for the why and the placement rules, research/refs.md for the photo pipeline, and research/scenes.md for the scene cards.

369 items: 171 outdoors, 198 indoors. How sure: 32 seen on the dated photos, 105 sourced, 203 to confirm on the matched photos, 29 doubtful (don't build until settled). Priority 1 (every street or room of its kind): 121.

Columns: **conf** is seen (on dated photos, with how many), src (a source says so), photo (to confirm on the matches) or check (doubtful). **build**: solid (parametric raea solid), blender (script), gen (image model, then Idol), decal (texture from a period print or photo), cloth, plant, grammar (part of the street or room grammar), char (worn or carried), fx. **p**: 1 everywhere, 2 common, 3 particular places, 4 rare. **refs**: the best matched photo.
