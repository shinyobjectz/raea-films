v3 is the film started over after the director's notes on assembly 8 (all verbatim in brief.ts). Its idea: **a place is a
drawing until the cursor touches it.** No words until the card. Source is `v3/`; `v2/` is the rejected film, kept.

The rooms of the walk score close to one another (footsteps and doors in all of them), which the test can't separate; the bedroom's tone is too quiet for it to judge. This is a check that each sound is the right kind of sound, not of how the mix feels.

## Tools

`bash tools/v3.sh [ids]` renders shots and assembles `out/v3/film.mp4`; `tools/strip3.sh ID` a dense strip of a shot;
`raea check out/v3/film.mp4` (clean on assembly 5); `bun stay.checks.ts`.
