# Swamp: credits

The film has no titles, so its credits travel with it here and on its post. Everything below is real material made by
people (scans, recordings, sampled instruments, maps); none of it is generated. Each folder's own SOURCE/LICENSE note
has the links and the fetch dates.

## Credit required (CC BY)

- **Flying Raven** by Google Data Arts Team, CC BY 3.0, via Poly Pizza (https://poly.pizza/m/p4puYPVor6): the ravens
  fleeing the trees. assets/models/raven/LICENSE.md
- **Salamander Grand Piano V3** by Alexander Holm, CC BY 3.0: the score's piano. assets/instruments/salamander
- **Alopochen aegyptiaca skull photogrametric scan** by the Musée national d'histoire naturelle Luxembourg (MNHNL),
  CC BY 4.0 (https://data.public.lu/en/datasets/alopochen-aegyptiaca-skull-photogrametric-scan-test/): the goose's
  skull in the mud behind the keyboard; decimated and scaled. assets/models/goose/SOURCE.md

## Public domain and CC0, credited with thanks

- Stars: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio, Ernie Wright, **Deep Star Maps 2020** (from Hipparcos-2, Tycho-2 and
  Gaia DR2). assets/textures/stars/SOURCE.md
- The Earth: NASA Earth Observatory and NASA GSFC, **Blue Marble: Next Generation** (Reto Stöckli; clouds enhanced by
  Robert Simmon), **Black Marble 2016** (Joshua Stevens; Suomi NPP VIIRS data from Miguel Román), and GEBCO 2008
  relief. assets/textures/earth/SOURCES.md

- The swamp: scans, HDRI skies and textures from **Poly Haven** and **ambientCG** (CC0). assets/models, assets/hdri,
  assets/textures
- Every recorded sound: CC0 recordings from **freesound.org**, each author listed in assets/audio/real/SOURCES.md,
  assets/audio/v3/SOURCES.md, assets/audio/v4/SOURCES.md and assets/audio/v5/SOURCES.md.
