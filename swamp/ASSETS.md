# Assets and their licences

The code in this repository and every asset made for this film are **Apache-2.0** (../LICENSE). The assets made by
others keep their own licences, listed here. Almost all are **CC0** (public domain dedication: use them for anything,
no credit needed, though it's thanked below). Three are **CC BY** and need their credit kept wherever you use them,
and NASA's imagery is public domain. Each folder of `assets/` (from `tools/get-assets.sh`) carries its own SOURCE,
LICENSE or `.json` sidecar with the same details and the date it was fetched.

The raea name and logo (`assets/brand/`) are trademarks and are not licensed under Apache-2.0 (its section 6): put
your own mark in their place if you make your own film from this.

## Made for this film (Apache-2.0)

Everything else in `assets/` was made for this film, by the code in this repository:

| Assets | Made by |
|---|---|
| `models/ivy`, `models/leafburst` | `scripts/make_ivy.py`, `scripts/make_leafburst.py` (grown from the LeafSet029 scans) |
| `models/mothership` | `solids/mothership.ts` (a raea solid), fitted out by `blender/mothership.py` |
| `textures/skin` (the aliens' hands) | `scripts/make_skin.py` |
| `textures/grime.png` (the keyboard's grime) | `scripts/make_grime.py` |
| `room/` (the review room's screen and the alien script) | `scripts/room_screen.py`, from this film's own frames |
| `plates/` (the dead, alive, dusk, mist, sun and water plates) | rendered by raea from this film's own scenes (each `.render.json` says how) |
| `app-take/` (the app on the monitor) | the raea app driven by `app-take/fixture` (a mock API and agent) and captured state by state by `scripts/app_states.py` |
| `audio/v3`, `audio/v4`, `audio/v5` (the effects and the score) | `scripts/sfx_build_*.py`, `scripts/alien_voice_v3.py`, `scripts/surfacing_v5.py`, `scripts/film_room_v5.py`, `scripts/into_white_v5.py` and `scripts/score_v5.py`, from the recordings and instruments above |
