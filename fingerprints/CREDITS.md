# Fingerprint: credits

Everything in the film is a raea render, drawn in code, or real material under the licence below. Nothing in the
picture is generated. Each folder's LICENCE.txt has its source.

## Picture

- **Coffee footage and the prop photos** (scissors, magnifying glass, film camera): Pixabay, under the Pixabay
  Content License. `media/stock`, `media/objects`.
- **Paper and card textures**: ambientCG (Paper001 to Paper006, Cardboard001, Cardboard004), CC0 1.0. `media/textures`.
- **Fonts**: Caveat, Reenie Beanie and DM Serif Display (SIL Open Font License 1.1); Special Elite and Permanent Marker
  (Apache 2.0); from Google Fonts. `media/fonts`.
- **The raea logo**: raea's own file, `media/brand/raea-logo.png`, not redrawn.
- **Drawing**: every stroke (the eye, the fingerprint, the laptop, the arrows, the handwriting's motion) is generated
  in code in `parts/` with perfect-freehand (MIT) and SVG filters that raea runs as GPU passes.

## Sound

- **Voice**: Fish Audio (drama-3, the "Explainer" voice), timed word by word with parakeet-mlx.
- **Foley**: raea's local Stable Audio Open takes (`raea sao`), `media/sfx`.
- **Score** (`tools/compose.ts`, `media/score/score.mid`), played on:
  - FluidR3 GM (celesta, pizzicato strings, acoustic bass, string ensemble) by Frank Wen, rendered by Benjamin
    Gleitzman (github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts), **CC-BY 3.0**.
  - tonejs-instruments (clarinet, bassoon) by Nicholaus Brosowsky (github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments),
    public domain and CC-BY sources per its README.
  - Versilian Community Sample Library (upright piano, marimba, glockenspiel, woodblock, shaker, cabasa, bass drum,
    mark trees, triangle, finger cymbals, Nepalese hand bells, ratchet, slit drum), CC0.

## What the film shows of Fingerprint

The flags and verdicts on screen are what `raea fingerprint score` said of the test cuts in `media/cuts` (built by
`tools/testcuts.sh`): "too dark 0:07" and "hiss 0:04" on cut-v1, "still there" for the hiss turned down to a third
(cut-v2), and both fixed in cut-v3. Its record is in `media/cuts/score-*.txt`.

# Fingerprint v3 (the splat film, `fingerprint3.tsx`): credits

Everything in the picture is a gaussian splat drawn by raea's `<Splat>`. None was generated by an image or video
model. Two were trained from real footage with raea (`raea splat train`: COLMAP, then Brush on Metal). `tools/splats.ts`
writes the rest (`v3/gs.ts` is the writer): some sampled from real scans and frames, some grown from mathematics.

## Picture

- **The farmhouse** (your footage, inside the machine) and **the laughing Buddha**: splats trained with raea, from
  raea's own `content/anything/splat/media` (orbit.ply, buddha.ply). The farmhouse is from Pixabay video 307719 by
  aerofotografialincoln, the Buddha from Pixabay video 101701 by KlausHausmann, both under the Pixabay Content License.
- **The hand**: the director's purchased scan (White Male 60s Hand), its surface sampled with its own 8K colour map. The scan
  stays out of git (`media/hand/`, see `.gitignore`); its licence doesn't allow sharing the files.
- **Camera, the two vases**: Poly Haven scans (Camera 01, Ceramic Vase 01, Brass Vase 01), CC0, sampled with their
  textures. `media/set/*.glb.json` has each one's source.
- **The film helix**: 36 frames of the project's own test cut (`media/cuts/cut-v1.mp4`, and `cut-v3.mp4` for the fixed
  frames), built from Pixabay stock footage under the Pixabay Content License.
- **The cat**: raea's own model (`content/cat3d/media/cat-white.glb`).
- **Dust, brain, neurons, attractor (Lorenz), paint ribbons, fingerprint whorl, galaxy, the machine's frame, stars**:
  written in code.
- **Fonts**: Instrument Serif and IBM Plex Mono (SIL Open Font License 1.1), from Google Fonts.
- **The raea logo**: raea's own file, `media/brand/raea-logo.png`.

## Sound

- **Voice**: Fish Audio, the voice the director chose (drama-3), timed word by word with parakeet-mlx. `tools/voice3.ts`.
- **Score** (`tools/compose3.ts`, played by `v3/score.tsx`):
  - tonejs-instruments (harmonium, contrabass, cello) by Nicholaus Brosowsky
    (github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments), public domain and CC-BY sources per its README (the cello is CC-BY 3.0
    by flcellogrl on Freesound).
  - Versilian Community Sample Library (kalimba, wine glasses, vibraphone, psaltery, shaker, woodblock, triangle,
    finger cymbals, mark trees, hand bell, ratchet), CC0.
- **Effects**: raea's local Stable Audio Open takes (`raea sao`): the pop, the shimmer, the cat.

## What the film says of Fingerprint

Each claim is from `docs/fingerprint.md`: it learns from approvals, picks and notes, in the person's words; it flags
moments before the person sees a cut; it tells a fix from a disguise; it runs on the person's machine; a team shares
what it learned, never media. "Too dark 0:07" and "hiss 0:04" are what `raea fingerprint score` said of the test cut.
