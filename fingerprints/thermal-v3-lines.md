# Fingerprint film: v3 lines (draft)

The director, 11-13: "it should be educational about what we are doing and the models and systems underneath without being
technical". The voice says it plainly; the model's real name rides on screen as a small HUD tag, so the curious can
look it up and nobody has to parse it. Every claim is from docs/fingerprint.md.

**New** lines are marked. Everything else is already voiced (Adrian) and stays. About 2:05 in all (X's cap is 2:20).
